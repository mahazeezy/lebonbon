Questions
Everything you
need to know.
Where are you located? +
We're at 908 Ogden Ave, Unit A, Lisle, IL 60532 — close to the Lisle post office with ample free parking. Easy to find, easy to park.
What are your hours? +
We're open Monday through Saturday, 11:00 AM – 9:00 PM. Closed Sundays. If you're placing a larger order, call ahead so we can have everything ready for you.
Is all your food Halal? +
Yes — 100% Halal, always. Every protein we serve meets strict Halal certification standards, no exceptions. We also have vegan and gluten-free options across our menu.
Do you offer delivery? +
Yes! We're on DoorDash and Uber Eats for delivery. You can also call (630) 822-4086 directly for takeout or curbside pickup at our Lisle location.
What's the Bon Bon Sampler? +
It's our tasting board — 1 beef brochette, 1 chicken brochette, 1 lamb chop, chicken wings, rice, salad, and plantains. Bon Bon Juice is included. Perfect for first-timers or anyone who wants to try it all.
Do you have vegan or gluten-free options? +
Yes. Our alloco (fried plantains), vegan jollof rice, and home salad are all vegan-friendly. Ask us when you order about gluten-free accommodations — we're happy to help.
FAQ

We're happy to answer anything. Call, email, or stop by — we respond fast and we're always friendly.
